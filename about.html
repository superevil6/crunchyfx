Open CrunchySFX, the sound effect generator

Free CC0 sound effect generator

CrunchySFX makes sound effects for games, in your browser: coins, jumps, lasers, explosions, hits, UI clicks, footsteps, magic, monsters, robot voices, bells and drones. Pick one of 900+ presets in 30+ categories or press Randomize, shape it with plain-language sliders or the full synth, and export a WAV. Breed nine offspring of a sound and keep the ones you like, build new ones by kind and console style in the Foundry, or make a pack of variations so a sound you play a hundred times never repeats exactly.

It is made for game developers who need their sounds now and don't want to dig through sample sites or worry about licenses. A WAV drops straight into Godot, Unity, GameMaker or any engine: 22.05, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 16-bit or 24-bit, stereo or mono, with an optional loop point. A Sound Kit makes a whole set for your game in one era at once, and any sound can be shared as a link that opens it again exactly.

Every sound is synthesized from scratch by hand-written DSP code, with 20+ engines: classic oscillators, Karplus-Strong plucks, modal bells, granular clouds, additive pads, 4-operator FM, formant voices and text-to-speech, and impact and whoosh foley. There are no samples and no AI in the app, and every sound is rendered on your own machine. Every sound you create with CrunchySFX is CC0: use it in anything, commercial included, with no attribution. Audio you import or record yourself keeps its own license.

The web version is free and has every engine and tool. The desktop app on itch.io ($6, Windows and Linux) adds workflow around the same sounds: it saves them straight into your game's folder, updates them in place, keeps a Library and your own presets, and opens and saves project files.

Questions

Is CrunchySFX free for commercial use?

Yes. Every sound you create or export with CrunchySFX is CC0 1.0, which puts it in the public domain: use it in commercial games, videos and apps, with no attribution and no royalties. Audio you import yourself keeps its own license. The web version is free.

Does CrunchySFX use AI or samples?

No. Every sound is synthesized from scratch by hand-written DSP code: oscillators, filters, plucked strings, bell resonators, FM, granular clouds and formant voices. There is no AI model and no sample library in the app.

How do I use the sounds in Godot or Unity?

Export a WAV and drop it in. In Godot 4, put it in res:// and play it with an AudioStreamPlayer; in Unity, put it in Assets/ and play it with an AudioSource. Variation packs and Sound Kits download as a zip of WAVs with a README that says the same.

What can it export?

WAV files at 22.05, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 16-bit or 24-bit, stereo or mono, with an optional loop point for ambience and drones. A sound can also be shared as a link that recreates it exactly.

How is it different from sfxr or bfxr?

sfxr and bfxr are great, and their sounds are free to use too.
